Before you film

Filming guide

Two short videos for the Go Rob Lacy dealer page. The VSL plays at the top of the landing page. The confirmation video plays on the page people see right after they book their free build call. Rob or David can film either one alone, or both of you can film together.

Non-negotiables

  1. Use the best camera you have. This needs to be the highest quality possible. A recent phone in 4K is fine.
  2. USE A MICROPHONE. A clip-on lav mic on your shirt, or a shotgun mic just out of frame. Never the camera's built-in mic.
  3. Film horizontal, 16:9. Both videos play in a wide frame on the website.
  4. Recreate the setup above. Sit at a desk and talk to the camera, at the same distance as the photo.
  5. Talk like you're talking to a friend. Actually speak to them. Don't go too salesy. Watch the video on site.trainandscale.com before you film.
  6. Take your time. Don't rush or get frustrated. If you think you can do a line better, repeat it until it's your best.
  7. Wear black. Black business-professional for the VSL; dark or black clothes for the confirmation video.
  8. Clean, professional background. Nothing cluttered or distracting behind you.
  9. Use the grid on your camera. Line your eyes up on the line shown below.

Set up the shot

  • Camera on a tripod at eye level, about arm's length to a few feet away, so you're framed from the chest up with a little desk visible.
  • Light your face from the front and slightly to one side: a window or a soft light at about 45°. Keep bright windows out from behind you.
  • Turn off fans and air conditioning, and silence phones. Check the mic level with a test clip before the first take.
  • Look into the lens, not at the screen.

Solo or together

  • Filming alone: read every section yourself. Say your own name where it says ACTOR FIRST NAME and your partner's name where it says PARTNER FIRST NAME.
  • Filming together: decide who is Presenter 1 and who is Presenter 2. Follow the Presenter 1 / Presenter 2 tags, and use the Together lines instead of the Solo lines.
  • In the together lines, each of you always says your own first name.

How to read the scripts

  • Normal text is spoken. Grey italic lines under each heading are directions. Don't say them.
  • The times next to each section are a guide to pacing, not a hard limit.
  • Film one section at a time. Leave two seconds of silence before and after each take so the edit is clean.

Say the offer exactly as written

  • The website, CRM, SMS automations and email automations are built free, with no obligation.
  • Don't add prices, results, numbers or promises that aren't in the script.
  • If a line doesn't feel natural, ask before changing what it promises. Small wording changes are fine.

Send the original video files (through Google Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer, not a text message, which compresses them). Send every take; the best takes are cut together in the edit.
